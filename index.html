★ Flagship Project

ClksOptimal

An AI SaaS platform for content creators

ClksOptimal is the biggest thing I've built — a product with a business behind it, not just a codebase. It's a subscription platform that helps creators win the part of the job that decides whether a video gets watched at all: the title and the thumbnail. It generates AI-driven video titles, analyzes thumbnails, and scores click-through-rate potential before a creator ever hits publish.

I founded it and built it end to end — the AI pipeline, the product itself, and the business plumbing underneath it, including Stripe for tiered pricing and recurring subscription billing. Shipping it meant learning the parts of software that don't show up in a class: real payments, real users, real uptime.
